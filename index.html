Reasonable Doubts

The questions that come up on a first call.

We already have a designer. Can you just build it?

Yes, if the design is finished and you own the files. We will tell you where it is likely to break on a phone before we start, and we will not quietly redesign it. Build-only work is quoted separately.

What happens to our search rankings?

We crawl the existing site first, map every URL, and ship redirects with the launch. We send you the before-and-after crawl so you can check the result rather than take our word for it.

Who owns the code?

You do. Work happens in a repository in your organisation, so if we stopped mid-project another studio could pick it up from the commit history.

Can our team edit it afterwards?

That is the point. Pages are assembled from named blocks in the CMS, and we train whoever will actually do the editing, not just the person who signed off.

How long does it take?

A week, from agreed brief to a live site. We settle the scope and the fee on the phone first, so the week is spent building rather than deciding.

How do we find out what it costs?

Call (612) 289-9506. We scope the work on the phone and quote a fixed fee before anything starts, so there is no hourly surprise at the end.
